## Problem section

### English

Founders often enter investor meetings trying to sell the project. Investors are often evaluating the person behind the project.

VCReady exists for the gap between those two mindsets.

### Chinese

很多早期创业者以为自己在向投资人介绍项目，但投资人更常在判断面前这个人是否真的理解问题、用户、风险和下一步。

VCReady 解决的就是这层认知错位。
